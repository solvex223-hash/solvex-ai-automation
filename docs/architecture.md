# SolveX Lead Capture & CRM Automation

## Project Overview

The SolveX Lead Capture & CRM Automation is a business workflow
designed to capture customer enquiries, organize lead information,
and notify the SolveX team automatically.

## System Architecture

The system uses the following workflow:

1. Tally — Collects business enquiries through an online form.
2. Make.com — Receives submissions and processes the workflow.
3. Google Sheets — Stores and manages lead records.
4. Gmail — Sends email notifications when new enquiries arrive.

## Lead Management

The CRM supports:

- Lead information management
- Project budget tracking
- Lead status tracking
- Follow-up scheduling
- Follow-up dates and last-contacted tracking
- Dashboard reporting

## Workflow

Tally Form
    ↓
Make.com Webhook
    ↓
Google Sheets CRM
    ↓
Gmail Notification
